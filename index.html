The story behind the name

Every good project is a bridge

Pontix comes from the Latin pons, pontis: bridge. The Romans were famous for them: sturdy, elegant structures that connected places and stood for centuries.

That is exactly what I want to build. On one side there is your business: its goals, its people, its constraints. On the other side there is the cloud, full of possibilities and full of complexity. Pontix spans the gap between the two, so you can cross safely and keep moving.

pons, pontis

  2. a connection between two places that were apart
  3. + x: the unknown I help you solve

About me

I'm the founder of Pontix and the person you'll work with, from the first conversation to the final handover. No account managers, no juniors in between: just direct access to an experienced consultant.

What I do
